This Harvard Professor Says AI Alignment Will BACKFIRE — Dr. Stephen Casper

Stephen Casper — MIT PhD, incoming Harvard Kennedy School professor — argues alignment research paradoxically backfires: it makes dangerous systems just safe enough to justify deploying them, while the real risk is a handful of labs concentrating control over transformative AI. His p(doom) is 5–10%, but his core fear is "gradual disempowerment" — most of humanity economically marginalized — and he wants decades-long, nuclear-style governance: audits, transparency, taxes on frontier labs.
