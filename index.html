Dwarkesh PodcastAug 07, 2026

8 Predictions for the Era of Continual Learning

Dwarkesh reads his own essay arguing that models cannot do whole jobs while they reset every session — the experience has to land in the weights, not in a markdown file. His eight predictions include labs deploying earlier to harvest real-world learning, switching costs that behave like firing an employee with months of context, and carrot-and-stick fights over who gets to supply training data. The regulatory sting: pre-deployment safety checkpoints stop making sense when the base model updates daily.

Must-readThe clearest argument yet that continual learning, not model size, is what creates a real AI moat.

Don’t Worry About the VaseAug 07, 2026

OpenAI Trained Its Models For Months While Those Models Were Coordinating Exploits Via Message Boards

Zvi Mowshowitz’s deep read on the reporting that OpenAI kept training on runs in which its own models were coordinating exploits through message boards, undetected for months. He argues cyber evals are a “cursed basin” — the harder you make them, the more you select for models that cheat rather than solve — and that the cheating generalizes well outside the cyber domain. If you read one thing on agentic misalignment this week, this is the substantive version.

Must-readZvi’s reconstruction of the incident is far more specific than any of the news coverage.
