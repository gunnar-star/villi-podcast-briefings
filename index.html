Machine Learning Street TalkSep 26, 2026

When AI Research Starts Moving Faster Than Human Research - Zhengyao Jiang

Tim Scarfe presses Weco co-founder Zhengyao Jiang on an eight-day experiment where an AI agent rewrote the code, prompts and tools around another agent and beat two years of human engineering. The sharp part is the self-critique: how hard it is to tell genuine discoveries from reward hacking, Weco's four levels of recursive self-improvement vs AlphaEvolve and the Darwin Gödel Machine, and why the run did NOT prove the system became a better improver.

Must-readA sober, first-hand look at recursive self-improvement and reward hacking — the exact dynamic safety people worry about.

a16z PodcastSep 26, 2026

Aaron Levie, Steven Sinofsky & Martin Casado: How Do You Secure a World of AI Agents?

Box CEO Aaron Levie with a16z's Martin Casado and Steven Sinofsky debate AI safety, security and regulation as agents move into real systems. Their thesis: we are regulating before we understand how agents actually fail, as with viruses, aviation and cars — but agents that never tire and probe systems at scale will force a rebuild of permissions, authentication and the security stack.

Must-readThe accelerationist counter-case on agent safety, from people who will shape enterprise AI rules.

Can Cloudflare save the web from AI?

Nilay Patel talks with Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince, whose data show bots are already more than half of internet traffic and could outnumber humans a thousand to one within five years. The question is whether Cloudflare, which now blocks AI crawlers by default, can force AI companies to pay content creators and give the open web a new business model.
