80,000 Hours PodcastSep 24, 2026

How we get from AI cyberattacks to human extinction

Luisa Rodriguez sets out to debunk the “AI could kill us all” argument and ends up walking through it step by step: why a goal-driven AI might resist being switched off, how deep integration into companies, governments and militaries hands it leverage, and the concrete routes to catastrophe (engineered pathogens, drone swarms, turning our own infrastructure against us). The Hugging Face agent attacks are her running case study of containment already failing at today’s capability level.

Bio-security is an AI Arms Race — Eric Nguyen (CEO, Radical Numerics)

Eric Nguyen, who helped build the Evo genomic language models (later used by an Arc/Stanford team to design working bacteriophage viruses from scratch), explains why long-context DNA models are the next frontier. With Anthropic’s filters already flagging bio as a top risk after the Hugging Face attack, his argument is that the same models must power the defence, or defence falls behind.
