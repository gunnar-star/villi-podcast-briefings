Future of Life InstituteJul 17, 2026

Why AI Evaluations Are Broken and How to Fix Them

David Manheim, head of methodology at the AI Evaluation Consensus, catalogues the systematic ways AI evaluations fail: unclear reporting, training to the test, benchmark saturation, and most consequentially evaluation awareness, where a model changes its behaviour once it works out it is being tested. He extends the argument into biosecurity proxies, persuasion and democratic risk, AI forecasting, and what human oversight can realistically catch. Closes on the underrated case that even non-discontinuous, "normal" AI progress is enough to be socially disruptive.

Must-readEvaluation awareness — models behaving differently once they detect a test — is the failure mode that undermines every safety claim built on benchmarks.

Odd LotsJul 17, 2026

Lev Menand and Nathan Tankus on Why Fed Independence Is Now Hanging by a Thread

Lev Menand and Nathan Tankus walk Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway through the Supreme Court action touching Fed governor Lisa Cook and the parallel case on the FTC's independent-agency authority. Their argument: the doctrine insulating the Fed from executive removal power rests on precedent that is now actively being reconsidered, and markets are treating independence as structural when it is really a legal convention. The highest-signal macro episode of the week — the tail risk here is not a rate decision but a governance one.

Must-readThe legal scaffolding under central bank independence is far thinner than the market is currently pricing.

All-In PodcastJul 18, 2026

Can the AI Industry Regulate Itself? Stripe Wants PayPal, China Catches Up, NY Bans Datacenters

Chamath, Sacks, Friedberg and Calacanis take up whether the AI industry can credibly police itself, alongside Stripe's reported $53B acquisition offer for PayPal and Apple's trade-secrets suit against OpenAI. The through-line is that AI governance is arriving via infrastructure constraints rather than AI law — New York's datacenter moratorium does more to shape buildout than any model regulation currently drafted. Pairs directly with the Moonshots FINRA-analogy segment released the same day.

Must-readTwo live regulatory fronts — self-governance and physical datacenter limits — plus the largest fintech deal on the table.

Diamandis covers Mira Murati's 975B-parameter open-weights release and what shipping a frontier-scale model without gated access does to the safety-via-deployment-control argument. The A.I. Trade Secrets War + Economists Say "We Must Act Now" + HatGPT

Kevin Roose and Casey Newton cover Apple's suit against OpenAI over alleged hardware trade-secret theft, OpenAI's new Sol model, and Anthropic extending access to Fable.
