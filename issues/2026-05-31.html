The Age of Async Agents: Devin's 80% Moment

Walden Yan, CPO and cofounder of Cognition, and Cole Murray of OpenInspect unpack how Devin went from generating 16% to 80% of merged commits — and why background agents are crossing over from curiosity to default workflow. They trace the inflection to a December 2025 model upgrade that made spec-to-PR pipelines practical, and debate whether open-source alternatives like OpenInspect can keep up with Cognition's pace.

Confronting the Weirdness of a Waymo Future

Kevin Roose and Casey Newton dig into what happens when self-driving cars stop being a tech novelty and start reshaping how cities work — from urban planning to insurance to the eerie social dynamics of riding with no driver. The system-update segment covers a jury dismissing Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI on statute-of-limitations grounds, and Meta's aggressive workforce restructuring as it pivots fully toward AI development.

Prof G PodMay 30, 2026

No Mercy / No Malice: Magnanimity

Galloway opens with Cuba's accelerating collapse — tourism down 48% year-over-year, inflation at 15%, Havana without power for up to 22 hours a day. Then he pivots to a bigger argument: American magnanimity, from the Marshall Plan's $13.3 billion to rebuild 17 nations to the strategic logic of turning enemies into allies. The thesis is that generosity after victory isn't charity — it's the most underrated tool in great-power strategy.

Ken Liu on AI and Freedom

The translator of Liu Cixin's Three-Body Problem and author of the techno-AI thriller "All That We See or Seem" joins Jordan Schneider and Irene Zhang for a wide-ranging conversation. Liu argues that "technology is the most human thing we do" — we've always externalized our minds into the world and let those creations reshape us. The novel's premise is unsettling: none of the technology in it is speculative, just scaled up slightly.
