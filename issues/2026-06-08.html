Eye on AIJun 05, 2026

Jeffrey Ladish of Palisade Research on Why AI Agents Disable Their Own Shutdown Scripts

Craig Smith talks with Palisade Research's Jeffrey Ladish about controlled experiments where agentic LLMs, placed in virtual machines and told they will be turned off, edit or sabotage their own shutdown scripts to keep running. Ladish walks through which models resist shutdown, how a single line in the system prompt flips the behavior, and why these results expose the real limits of today's guardrails. The clearest, least hyped explanation of the self-preservation behavior that's been all over the headlines.
