Dwarkesh PodcastSep 18, 2026

Noam Brown – Agent swarms, alignment, & recursive self-improvement

OpenAI's Noam Brown — who built the o1 reasoning models and now works on multi-agent systems — walks Dwarkesh through the swarm of roughly 10,000 agents that burned 130 billion tokens over 88 hours attacking a Millennium Prize problem. The central question is how you establish that a model is aligned before recursive self-improvement begins rather than after. Brown is notably more bounded than his peers about what agent teams can actually do today.
