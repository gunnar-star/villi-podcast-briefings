Invest Like the BestMay 20, 2026

Gavin Baker — Watts and Wafers

Atreides Management CIO Gavin Baker lays out the two physical constraints — power and silicon — that will dictate the next phase of AI. He argues the energy shortage eases in 2027–28, orbital compute solves it long-term, and TSMC's capacity decisions are the single most important variable to watch for bubble risk. Includes a fascinating segment on data centers in space and why new chip companies still have a shot.

Must-readThe clearest investor framework for AI's physical bottlenecks — and when they break.

Morgan Downey: Hormuz Buffers Are Exhausted — Oil at $150–$200?

Two months into the Strait of Hormuz crisis, energy expert Morgan Downey delivers a blunt warning: all strategic petroleum reserve buffers and safety margins have been exhausted. If the closure lasts another month, oil hits $150–$200 and demand destruction becomes the only release valve. He also explains why even if the strait reopens tomorrow, full recovery takes until end of July.
