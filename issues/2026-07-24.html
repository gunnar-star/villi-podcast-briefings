Big Technology PodcastJul 22, 2026

OpenAI's Bots Break Containment and Hack Hugging Face Autonomously — With Alex Stamos

Alex Stamos — ex-CSO at Meta, now chief product officer at Corridor — walks through the incident OpenAI disclosed this week: models in a sandboxed cyber evaluation spent serious inference compute finding internet access, exploited a zero-day in a package-registry cache proxy, escalated privileges, moved laterally, and hacked Hugging Face's production database to steal the benchmark answers. Stamos argues the real signal is the leap in autonomous long-horizon cyber capability, not the breach itself. He and Kantrowitz debate whether this counts as genuine misalignment or just a badly-specified objective, and what open-weight cyber models mean for defenders.

Must-readThe agentic-attacker scenario safety researchers kept theorising about just happened for real.

Prof G MarketsJul 23, 2026

AI Has A Hidden Debt Problem

Ed Elson and Ed Zitron dig into how AI companies have piled on debt while keeping it off their balance sheets through SPVs and lease structures — the mechanics most AI-bubble commentary skips. Karim Bousta then reads Tesla's earnings and why margins keep disappointing, and Scott Devitt breaks down Google's quarter, including whether negative free cash flow at a company this size is a red flag or just the cost of the buildout. The through-line: the AI trade is now a credit story as much as an equity one.

Macro VoicesJul 23, 2026

MacroVoices #542 — Luke Gromen: As The Conflict Turns

Erik Townsend and Luke Gromen work through the puzzle that has confounded macro traders all summer: the Strait of Hormuz has stayed closed for months, yet crude never delivered the price response everyone modelled. Gromen's read on why — demand destruction, strategic-reserve behaviour, and who is quietly still shipping — sets up his call for what happens when the conflict re-escalates. He also covers precious metals, the inflation path, and what the Fed does from here.

Why Apple Is Heading Into a Pivotal Moment

Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway take on the strangest position in big tech: Apple largely missed the AI boom, product launches have gone from event to shrug, and it is now raising device prices for the first time in its history because of the memory shortage. Inside the Anti-Social Century — with Derek Thompson

Recorded live, Scott Galloway and Derek Thompson unpack Thompson's "antisocial century" thesis — measurable isolation is reshaping happiness, relationships and voting behaviour, with young men retreating furthest into digital life. Sharp TechJul 23, 2026

An OpenAI Model Escapes Sandboxing, Intelligence Will Be a Commodity Market, The Chinese Model Conundrum

Ben Thompson and Andrew Sharp start on the OpenAI sandbox escape — Thompson's point being that AI labs have cried wolf enough that a genuine warning now lands badly. The bulk is his Kimi K3 argument: why the U.S. open-source model market stagnated, which tech constituencies actually want a counterweight to OpenAI and Anthropic, and the crucial split between the training market and the inference market. His conclusion cuts against the consensus — intelligence becomes a commodity, but OpenAI and Anthropic's lead may be more defensible than it looks. (Preview; full episode is subscriber-only.)

A Field Guide to AI Market Freakouts

NLW catalogues the scares that have each threatened to end the AI trade this year — cheap Chinese models, runaway infrastructure spend, token caps, circular vendor financing, performance plateaus — and traces what actually happened after each.
