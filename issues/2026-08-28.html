80,000 Hours PodcastAug 27, 2026

AI 2027's author returns with a plan to change the ending | Daniel Kokotajlo

Daniel Kokotajlo, whose AI 2027 scenario ended in either human extinction or an irreversible concentration of power, walks Luisa Rodriguez through the sequel: AI 2040: Plan A, in which Washington and Beijing strike a verified deal banning runaway intelligence explosions and superintelligence arrives in 2040 after a decade spent on alignment and reversibility. Even this “slowdown” assumes the economy roughly doubles every year and only 8% of Americans hold paid work by the mid-2030s. This is the long-form treatment of the Kokotajlo interview you got a shorter cut of on Tuesday — verification, enforcement, and the likeliest alternatives if Plan A fails.

Eye on AIAug 27, 2026

Inside Ukraine's Azov Drone R&D: The Engineer Building AI Weapons 18 km From the Front Line | Alexander Palamarchuk

Alexander Palamarchuk calls in from roughly 18 km from the Pokrovsk front to describe how the Azov Brigade's R&D unit builds and combat-tests AI-guided drones, and how the jamming war has pushed his team into custom frequency systems sweeping 100–3,000 MHz for windows Russia hasn't closed. His central distinction is the one policy debates keep missing: recognition is solved — AI classifies vehicles and people accurately today — but discrimination, knowing with certainty whether a recognised target is military or civilian, is not. He puts that gap at about two years from closing.

Must-readThe technical constraint on autonomous weapons has a stated expiry date.

Schmid previews the first Jackson Hole under new Fed chair Kevin Warsh — the speech every rates desk is positioning around — into a setup of high bond yields, above-target inflation, and an AI capex boom that still hasn't shown up in the labour data.
