Odd LotsJul 27, 2026

Branko Milanovic on What Comes After Globalization

Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway host economist Branko Milanovic (CUNY Stone Center) on his new book The Great Global Transformation, arguing that classic hyper-globalization is over and the world is fragmenting toward a “national/market liberalism” in which US–China rivalry, not free-trade orthodoxy, sets the terms. He traces how national interests are redrawing trade and capital flows — and what that leaves for a Europe caught in the middle.

How Much Money Is Enough? Plus, Why AI Won't Replace Financial Advisors — with Jack Raines

Scott Galloway hosts Jack Raines (Young Money) to interrogate what “enough” money actually means, why the rent-vs-buy math rarely favors buying in the most expensive cities, and what a financial advisor is really for once AI can execute the trades itself. The through-line: behavior and judgment, not access to information, are the durable human edge in personal finance.

TWIML AI PodcastJul 27, 2026

Why Models Are AI's Next Training Dataset (Ep. 772)

Host Sam Charrington talks with Damian Borth (University of St. Gallen) about “weight space learning” — treating the parameters of already-trained neural networks as data in their own right. Borth argues the distilled results of millions of GPU hours can be mined to build foundation models of models, potentially slashing the compute cost of producing specialized systems by learning from existing networks rather than training from scratch.
