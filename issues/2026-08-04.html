Don't Worry About the VaseAug 03, 2026

OpenAI's Unreleased Model Astra Solves Ten Major Open Mathematics Problems

Zvi Mowshowitz works through OpenAI's claim that its unreleased Astra model solved or materially advanced ten long-standing open maths problems for roughly $2,000 of compute — including problems the mathematicians who posed them had worked on for years. He grades how impressive the results actually are, whether anyone called this in advance, and what it should do to your timelines. The uncomfortable core: the field is producing verified results that almost nobody is qualified to check.

Must-readThe clearest read yet on whether the maths results are a genuine capability jump or a benchmark artifact.

Dwarkesh PodcastAug 03, 2026

Why smarter AI models could drive up compute prices 10x

Dwarkesh Patel narrates his argument that the usual assumption — better models mean cheaper intelligence — has it backwards: as models get smart enough to do economically valuable work, demand for inference outruns the efficiency gains and compute prices rise, perhaps tenfold. He walks through the elasticity maths that makes inference the binding constraint rather than training. Directly relevant to how you value anyone selling or renting GPUs.

Must-readA contrarian case that inference cost goes up, not down — with the numbers behind it.

Odd LotsAug 03, 2026

The Tungsten Market Is Warning of an Upcoming War

Bloomberg Opinion's David Fickling tells Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway that tungsten mining is effectively "a century-old prediction market for war" — the metal's density and melting point make it critical to munitions, and mines like Australia's Dolphin reopen when conflict looms and shutter when it passes. He explains why investors are suddenly interested again and why nobody has managed to sustain production outside China. A commodity almost no one tracks that is currently flashing.

Must-readA cheap, legible early-warning indicator for conflict risk hiding in an obscure metals market.
