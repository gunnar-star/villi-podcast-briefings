LLMs and the economics of Evil, Inc., with Manish Goregaokar

Patrick McKenzie and Rust security-response engineer Manish Goregaokar walk through a fraud built for professionals: a plausible fake company, several rounds of real-feeling interviews, and an NDA signed via "sign in with email" that hands the attacker the identity account every other password resets against. At least five people in the Rust community were hit within days of Manish publishing on it, and Patrick himself was targeted by someone posing as a Bloomberg reporter. They then cost out money mules, the three stages of laundering, and the $25m Hong Kong transfer authorised on a deepfaked video call.
