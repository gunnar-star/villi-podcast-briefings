Is There An AI Kill Switch If Things Go Wrong?

Miles Brundage — ex-OpenAI policy lead, now running the non-profit AVERI — tells Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway why the "we can always pull the plug" assumption doesn't survive contact with how these systems actually get deployed. Ben Thompson of Stratechery walks Patrick O'Shaughnessy through the whole board — OpenAI, Nvidia, Intel, Apple, Microsoft, Google, Amazon — with the central question being where the AI capex cycle hits a funding wall. Why China's AI Bubble Is Also Industrial Policy

Leia Wang (Carnegie Endowment / GovAI) makes the case on ChinaTalk that what looks like undisciplined Chinese AI overinvestment is functioning as deliberate state industrial policy — the bubble is the mechanism, not the accident. She anchors it in the collapse of foreign participation in Chinese VC, from 54.8% of deal value in 2018 to roughly 20% by 2023, accelerated by the 2023 US outbound-investment order.

Worth a lookReframes "China's AI bubble" as policy working as intended rather than capital misallocation.
