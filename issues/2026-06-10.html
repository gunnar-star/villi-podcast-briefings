Dan Dreyfus: The Next AI Bottleneck is Copper

Mining veteran Dan Dreyfus argues copper, not GPUs, is the next hard ceiling on the AI buildout, as datacenters, grids and electrification collide with a decade of underinvestment in supply. He explains why America's "Capital Light Era" is ending, how China's grip on refining hands it real leverage, and what a structural supply shock would mean for prices. Essential framing for the physical constraints sitting underneath the entire AI trade.

Bill Maris: How Google Could Crush AI Competitors, Why Small Funds Win, and AI's Atari Stage

Google Ventures founder Bill Maris explains how GV ran millions of machine-learning simulations to engineer portfolios that beat the VC averages, and why small funds consistently outperform mega-funds with broken incentives. He makes a pointed case that OpenAI has a valuation problem as an AI price war erupts, and frames today's models as still being at the "Atari stage" of a far longer arc. A rare, numbers-driven look at venture mechanics from one of its sharpest operators.

Nikesh Arora: Cybersecurity in the Age of AI and the Path to $1 Trillion

Palo Alto Networks CEO Nikesh Arora details how AI is rewriting cybersecurity, citing Claude uncovering five to six years' worth of code vulnerabilities in six weeks, and debates whether defenders can keep pace with AI-enabled attackers. He argues analytical SaaS is effectively dead, rates Waymo, Google and OpenAI in an "Armchair CEO" segment, and lays out the M&A playbook toward a $1T valuation. A clear-eyed view from the company defending one of the largest attack surfaces in tech.

Kris Lovejoy: Why Agentic AI Is a Bullet Train on 30-MPH Tracks
