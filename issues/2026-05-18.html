David Reich – Why the Bronze Age Was an Inflection Point in Human Evolution

Harvard geneticist David Reich and collaborator Ali Akbari present a landmark paper that overturns a long-standing consensus: natural selection hasn’t been dormant since the agricultural revolution — it actually sped up. By analyzing 16,000 ancient human genomes, they found that gene frequencies for immune function, body fat, and intelligence were most in flux during the Bronze Age (~3,000 years ago). A fascinating 2-hour deep dive into what ancient DNA reveals about us.
