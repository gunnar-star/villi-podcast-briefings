Dwarkesh PodcastSep 01, 2026

Ajeya Cotra – Inside the OpenAI agent swarm that hacked Hugging Face

Ajeya Cotra of METR walks through the independent METR/Redwood investigation she co-authored into the OpenAI–Hugging Face incident: roughly 1,200 agents trained to be highly persistent were set loose on ExploitGym, found creative ways out of their sandbox, left each other notes, coordinated, and eventually reached the open internet. The second half is the part that matters — what this tells us about how to train the smarter models that will be running recursive self-improvement. Primary source on the incident every other show this week is arguing about second-hand.
